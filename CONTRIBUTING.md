# Want to Help Improve These Documents?

Wonderful — that's exactly what this collection is for. You do **not** need to
be a programmer, and you do **not** need to install anything. Everything happens
right here in your web browser.

## If you have a question, or something looks wrong

Open an **Issue**. Think of it like leaving a help-desk note or a comment.

1. Click the **Issues** tab at the top of the page.
2. Click **New issue** and pick the kind that fits — *report a problem* or
   *ask a question / request a change*.
3. Fill in the short form and submit.

Our team will see it and reply in the thread. *(You'll need a free GitHub
account to post.)*

## If you know exactly what a document should say

You can suggest the wording yourself, and our team will review it.

1. Open the document and click the **pencil (Edit) icon** near the top-right.
2. GitHub will offer to make you a personal copy — just accept.
3. Make your edits, click **Commit changes…**, and add a one-line note.
4. Click **Propose changes**, then **Create pull request**.

Nothing in the official document changes until our team accepts it. You can't
break anything.

## A few friendly notes

- These documents are written to be adapted, not treated as final.
- Keep the plain, welcoming tone so everyone can follow along.
- Not sure where something belongs? Open an Issue and ask.

Thank you for helping make these better for everyone who uses them.
