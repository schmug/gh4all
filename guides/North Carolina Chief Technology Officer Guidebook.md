## Expectations of a Chief Technology Officer/Technology Director

The Office of Digital Teaching and Learning supports the use of the [CoSN Framework of Essential Skills for Chief Technology Officers](https://www.cosn.org/careers-certification/framework-of-essential-skills/). This framework focuses on:

- Leadership & Vision
- Strategic Planning
- Ethics & Policies
- Instructional Focus & Professional Development
- Team Building & Staffing
- Information Technology Management
- Communication Systems Management
- Business Management
- Data Management
- Data Privacy and Security

Technology leaders who wish to learn more about these particular topics and become a CoSN Certified Educational Technology Leader, should contact [Dr. Ashley McBride](mailto:ashley.mcbride@dpi.nc.gov) to enroll in the annual immersion course. This course is offered in partnership with NCCoSN and paid for by NCDPI using Digital Learning Initiative Funds. Once the course has been completed, participants can take the exam during the pre-conference at NCTIES in March. Participants are responsible for securing funding for the exam itself.

### Student Information System Roles

The student information system (SIS) is crucial to the success of digital teaching and learning. 3rd party instructional tools will need to be integrated into your SIS to allow login and tracking of student performance. Each school in your system should have a SIS Data Manager. These data managers should be 11 or 12 month employees. High School data managers need to be 12-month full time employees.

Each PSU must have a person who is the SIS Coordinator. This person ensures compliance with state reports, and ensures that data is accurate and timely. In smaller PSUs this may be a duty assigned to another role such as Technology or Testing and Accountability.

### Digital Learning and Media Inventory

- The NC Digital Learning & Media Inventory (NC DLMI) collects data for state and national reporting, informs state and local budgets, and assists in planning state and local digital learning efforts.
- All PSUs (LEA, Charter, Regional, & Lab) are legislatively mandated to complete the DLMI for each School calendar year. The DLMI is due each year at the end of June.
- Data from the DLMI is used in:
  - School Report Card (annually)
    - NOTE: If data is not supplied via the DLMI, these data points will be missing from the School Report Card.
  - Civil Rights Data Reporting (normally biannually)
  - Digital Learning Dashboards
    - The Student Digital Learning questions have been added to the 2022-2023 collection to fulfill session law "§ 115C-102.9," which requires collection and reporting of quantity and type of student access to digital devices and home internet.
    - To assist PSUs in collecting the questions required in session law "§ 115C-102.9," an optional parent survey is available in the student information system in the Parent Portal. Use of the survey is optional and provided as a support. Each PSU will need to determine the best method to collect and report this information, but it is important to note that the survey in the SIS will automatically import into the DLMI, saving PSUs a lot of time and effort.
    - If PSUs choose NOT to utilize the SIS survey, they will need to collect the items using the exact questions and answer choices and aggregate them by GRADE at each school and then import or manually enter the data by grade on the School DLMI.
    - To access the DLMI online collection tool, go to <https://schools.nc.gov/ncdlmi>
