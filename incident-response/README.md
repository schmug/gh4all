# Incident Response

Documents that help your team **prepare for and respond when something goes
wrong** — an outage, a lost or stolen device, a mistaken disclosure, or any
other urgent situation.

**What belongs here**
- Step-by-step response checklists ("what to do in the first hour")
- Who-to-call contact and escalation lists (templates, not real numbers)
- Notification guidance (when and how to inform the people affected)
- After-the-fact review templates ("what happened and what we'll change")

**What does *not* belong here**
- General how-to guides → see [`guides/`](../guides/)
- Reusable fill-in-the-blank forms → see [`templates/`](../templates/)
- Reviewing outside companies and tools → see [`vendor-security/`](../vendor-security/)

These are living references. Review and adapt anything before adopting it for
your organization.
